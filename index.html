Why Local Counsel Matters in Seattle

Probate, guardianship, divorce, and parenting-plan matters are decided in county courts, and practice differs across King, Snohomish, and Pierce counties. An attorney who appears in those courtrooms regularly knows how a given commissioner runs a calendar and what local practice actually requires.
